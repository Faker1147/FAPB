Notes
  - The original YAPB aim/shooting/behaviour code is unchanged.
  - Only plugin/cmd/cvar/path strings were renamed (binary-level fork).
  - The team menu is an AMXX companion plugin; it calls fapb commands.
  - Hotkey binding is per-player (`bind <key> fapb_teams`).
  - Do not load FAPB and YAPB/SYPB as active bot cores at the same time.
